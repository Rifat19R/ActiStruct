## Scientific meaning

1. **Ni and Cr pseudopotentials CONFIRMED as correct SSSP 1.3.0 efficiency files.**
   - `ni_pbe_v1.4.uspp.F.UPF` = GBRV-1.4 Ultrasoft (Garrity, Bennett, Rabe, Vanderbilt).
   - `cr_pbe_v1.5.uspp.F.UPF` = GBRV-1.5 Ultrasoft.
   - The naming difference from `_psl.` is expected: SSSP deliberately selects from
     multiple source libraries (PSLibrary, GBRV, ONCV, …) per element. This is not an
     error. All 8 Ni/Cr DFT calculations used the correct pseudopotentials.

2. **Fe ecutwfc concern flagged (new finding, not in original task).**
   - SSSP efficiency recommends `ecutwfc = 90 Ry` for Fe (PAW-031).
   - Our benchmark used `ecutwfc = 60 Ry` throughout.
   - For isolated gas-phase molecules in large supercells the convergence cutoff can be
     lower than the SSSP solid-state recommendation, but this has NOT been verified.
   - Action: Rifat must run a cutoff convergence test (60, 75, 90 Ry) on ferrocene or
     Fe(CO)5 before citing Fe-system results in papers or presentations.
   - The pseudopotential file itself is correct (MD5 matches).

3. **All 16 dataset rows now carry `pseudo_verified=True`.**
   - This means the UPF files used are confirmed as authentic SSSP efficiency files.
   - It does NOT certify that the DFT settings (cutoffs) are fully converged.
