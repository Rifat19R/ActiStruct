## Scientific meaning

1. **Convergence confirmed**: PBE-D3 at 90 Ry relaxes cleanly in 12 BFGS steps.
2. **Conformer check PASSED (Task 3 partially resolved)**: Dihedral = 0.00° →
   eclipsed D5h ground state. This is the experimentally known ground state.
   The PBE-D3 geometry is consistent with literature.
3. **Fe–Cp centroid distance**: 1.634 Å. Literature PBE-D3 values are ~1.64–1.66 Å;
   our result is at the lower end but within typical functional variation.
4. **Dispersion effect**: D3 lowers total energy by 0.484 eV vs PBE baseline.
   Note: this ΔE conflates dispersion (physical) and cutoff change 60→90 Ry
   (~18.6 meV/atom from Task 1.5). A fair dispersion-only comparison would
   require PBE at 90 Ry, which is not run here (not needed for the blocker check).

## Known limitations

- ΔE(PBE-D3 − PBE) conflates dispersion + cutoff. Quantitative dispersion
  contribution not isolated.
- `vdw_corr = 'grimme-d3'` uses D3 zero-damping. Becke-Johnson damping
  (`grimme-d3bj`) is theoretically preferred but requires checking QE build support.
